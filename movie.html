Fight Club

About the Movie

Fight Club follows an insomniac office worker looking for a way to change his life. He crosses paths with a devil-may-care soap maker named Tyler Durden. Together, they form an underground fight club that evolves into a radical, anti-corporate organization known as Project Mayhem.

Main Characters

  • The Narrator – An unfulfilled everyday worker suffering from severe insomnia.
  • Tyler Durden – A charismatic, free-spirited soap salesman.
  • Marla Singer – A cynical woman who disrupts the Narrator's support group routine.
  • Robert "Bob" Paulson – A former bodybuilder who joins the fight club support circle.
  • Angel Face – A young, loyal recruit of Project Mayhem.

Movie Timeline

  1. The Flight: The Narrator meets the enigmatic soap salesman Tyler Durden during a routine business trip flight.
  2. The Apartment Fire: The Narrator returns home to find his condominium blown up, leaving him homeless.
  3. The First Fight: Out of options, the Narrator calls Tyler, and they share a consensual fistfight outside a bar.
  4. The Club Expands: Their casual parking-lot brawls turn into a massive underground fight club network across the country.
  5. The Realization: The Narrator uncovers a shocking psychological truth about his own relationship with Tyler Durden.

Interesting Facts

Production Details

Director David Fincher secretly placed a Starbucks coffee cup in every single scene of the film as a subtle nod to consumer culture.

Character Prep

Brad Pitt and Edward Norton took actual boxing and taekwondo lessons to make their physical encounters look completely authentic on screen. (Fun Fact: Brad Pitt even had his front teeth chipped for the role!)
