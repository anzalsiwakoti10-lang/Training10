The DHAGO Story

Every Stitch Tells a Tale of Himalayan Artistry

Founded with a passion for preserving traditional Nepalese textile arts, DHAGO (धागो) bridges ancient handloom techniques with global streetwear fashion.

We work directly with women weavers in Palpa, Terhathum, and Mustang, providing fair wages and sustainable employment while ensuring ancient weaving traditions thrive in the modern age.
